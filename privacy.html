Privacy policy

rclone-backup and finance-fetch are each used by exactly one person: their owner, with his own Google accounts. This policy covers that use.

  • Data accessed, rclone-backup: only files this app created in the owner's Google Drive (drive.file scope). The files hold backups that are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload, so Google only ever stores ciphertext.
  • Data accessed, finance-fetch: read-only access to the owner's Gmail (gmail.readonly). It searches for statement emails from banks and financial institutions and saves their attachments on the owner's computer. Message content stays on that computer and is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Data collected: none. There are no servers, analytics, cookies, or third parties. Both apps run entirely on the owner's own computer.
  • Sharing: nothing is shared, sold, or transferred to anyone.
  • Retention and deletion: the owner controls the backups in his Drive and the files on his computer, and can delete them, or revoke either app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, at any time.
  • Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

rclone-backup

This page covers two personal, single-user Google OAuth clients. Each lives in its own Google Cloud project. Neither is a public service: no one else uses them, and they have no sign-up.

rclone-backup

An OAuth client for rclone. Its owner uses it to store end-to-end encrypted backups (made with restic) in his own Google Drive. It requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope, so it can see and manage only the files it created itself, not the rest of the Drive.

finance-fetch

A script on the owner's computer that finds bank, card and investment statement emails in his own Gmail accounts and downloads their attachments to that computer, for his personal bookkeeping. It requests only the read-only Gmail scope (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete mail.
